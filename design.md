# Synthesis
A mod idea I had.

# The Experience
The mod idea predicates itself on giving the player a task of collecting an astronomical amount of resources. 
It would create a unique new experience where the player strategizes the ideal means of collecting and hoarding tons of resources as they scavenge the whole map.

There are a lot of resources scattered all over the map, notably the drillables. And I feel that some resources are immensely abundant but don't see enough use for it, like uraninite, lead, and magnetite. And I feel like they could be given more significance for actually collecting a lot of it, because the usual player would never touch these things.  
It also offers an experience for whose like me who enjoy the grind.

Inspired by the Avaritia Minecraft mod, that is sort of what I was thinking.  
It shouldn't be complex with many crafting chains either; just bringing together a lot of raw resources en mass.

# The Reward
It doesn't matter to me what the reward actually would be for getting all these resources, but anything at all to get the player moving. 

And my idea for this became resource infinity.  
The notion is that "you gathered all these items, may as well have an infinite amount of it"
The idea grew into a power sink too. The game offers too easy of power generation, so achieveing infinity comes with a price. It would make nuclear reactors see more usefulness in a way that matters.

# The Logistics
So what might be the path to translate from hoarding into the reward of infinity? I'm thinking that the player would build a machine that synthesizes material out of thin air.

The player would accumulate an absurd amount of one resource like copper, and then would compress it to make a "Compressed Resource". The compressed resource would be the requirement to achieve infinity with that respective resource.

# The flow
- Player picks up an item that is required to build the synthesisizer and the compressor machine. Maybe Aerogel. This unlocks the tech.
- Player builds a synthesisizer outdoors. But it needs a compressed resource to operate, so it doesn't do anything. The player is then compelled to craft the compressor.
- Player builds the compressor indoors. They click through the arrows to set themselves on copper. The machine reads like the neptune rocket, where the ui is on world space.
- the compressor indicates how much resources is stored in the base plus player, and alsp how much is needed to craft the compressed resource.
- the compressed resource can not be slotted into the synthesizer, and it will begin fabricating ores, much like how the precursor tech works for making ion crystals.
